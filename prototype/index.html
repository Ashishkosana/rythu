Rythu — Prototype v2

Research-backed. Bhupalpally · Kharif · Telugu default.

  • Tap English (top-right) → whole app switches language (working i18n).
  • Onboarding asks water source (the #1 crop input) — no Aadhaar.
  • Weather = honest probability + "forecasts can be wrong" + Open-Meteo source (not "hyperlocal").
  • Crops = explainable "because" chips; paddy gated behind water; cotton first.
  • Market = a sell decision + MSP red-flag (paddy below MSP).
  • 🔊 = tap-to-hear (voice output, v1 non-negotiable).
  • Top chip: "We sell you nothing" — the commission-free wedge.

Live-verified free data: Open-Meteo (weather) + data.gov.in Agmarknet (mandi). Soil-card = blocked → district baseline.
